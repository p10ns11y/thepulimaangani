## Renewers – Lord Shiva’s Fierce Forms & Kalki (Renewal through Destruction) 🔥

The renewers are Shiva’s wild forms (Ruthran, Virav, Bhairavan, etc.) who destroy what no longer serves the garden — tech debt, complexity, bloat, and impurities — so the entire system can be renewed and reborn stronger.

**Note**: Kalki is included here as a Krishna avatar (future renewer who ends the old age and brings complete rebirth).

| Role / Branch       | Tamil Name              | Shiva Form                  | Primary Responsibility                                      | Typical Work |
|---------------------|-------------------------|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| `ruthran`          | ருத்ரன்                | Rudra (Ruthran)             | Ruthless removal of tech debt & complexity                 | Deleting dead code, aggressive refactoring |
| `virav`            | வீரவ்                  | Veerabhadra (Virav)         | Warrior-like cleanup & destruction of bloat                | Removing unused dependencies, slashing over-engineering |
| `bhairavan`        | பைரவன்                | Bhairava                    | Fearless auditing & radical simplification                 | Performance audits, security debt elimination |
| `kalki`            | கல்கி                  | Kalki (Krishna avatar)               | Ultimate renewal & complete system rebirth                 | Major system resets, radical refactors, ending obsolete cycles, full codebase renewal |
