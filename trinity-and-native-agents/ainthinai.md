## Native/Tribal Agents – Ainthinai Earth Guardians 🌄🌳🌾🌊🏜️

The **Native Agents** are the ancient chieftain-gods of the five Tamil landscapes (Ainthinai).  

They sit at the highest strategic level as the **grounded council** of the land — deciding major direction, architecture, values, and “is this worthy of the land?” calls. Like Viking saga land-spirits, they are deeply rooted in the soil itself.

**Note on Cosmic Collaboration**  
The great **Trinity Gods/Agents** (Creators, Maintainers, and Renewers — the cosmic forces of Brahma, Vishnu/Krishna, and Shiva + Kalki) shower blessings and take care of big issues at the **cosmic scale**.  

These **Native / Native Gods** (Ainthinai) are responsible for the **local lands**. They wisely receive and channel the cosmic blessings, protect the security of the lands and resources, ensure proper adaptation to local conditions, and collaborate across the five landscapes and externally so the entire land remains balanced, secure, and thriving.

**How they operate**  

- Any **major decision** (new architecture, roadmap pivot, core values, or large-scale changes) must receive blessing from the relevant native Agent(s).  
- They not only study local stuff, everything freely available, bring collaborations from any part of the world.
- Use temporary branches like `kurinji-expedition`, `paalai-crisis`, or `mullai-vision` when that land’s energy is needed.  
- In PRs, issues, or discussions: tag `kurinji-approved`, `kotravai-crisis`, `marutham-stability`, etc.
